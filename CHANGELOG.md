# Changelog

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-18): Beatswitch (this also contains the work first numbered 1.2.0)

**What is inside one track? Detune, key and tempo per section, and detune over time against tempo over time**: in the plugin (the new **beatswitch** button), in the library (`centsible/sections.h`) and on the command line. Documentation: `BEATSWITCH.md`; measurements: `REPORT.md` section 7.
- **All seven diatonic modes** (Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian): every key is now named with its mode (`C major (Ionian)`, `A minor (Aeolian)`, `D minor (Dorian) (7A)`), and the probability of each mode of the song's scale is reported (library `KeyEstimate::modeProbability`, `modeName`, `modeSummary`; the CLI prints `modes of this scale: Ionian 63 %, Aeolian 23 % ...`; the Beatswitch halves show the two most likely). The detector already chose among the seven tonics of the scale; they were just not all named.
- **New in 1.3.0 on the panel**: **mark switch** (live recording: press it at the moment the beat switches and its time goes into the cut field), the two halves of one cut shown **side by side** (first half | second half, then the difference), **use as cuts** (tempo mode: the suggested boundaries become the cuts of sections mode, one press), **copy results** (the results as plain text), and a **seek warning** (if the DAW's position jumps while recording, bar | beat times no longer fit: the panel says so).
- **The beatswitch button** (bottom row of the plugin) opens a panel over the plate. Source: the one file you dropped, or **live audio**: press record, play the track in your DAW, stop (the plugin keeps a copy on its analysis thread, never on the audio thread, together with the host's tempo, time signature and bar position). **Sections** mode: type the cut times (`0:45, 1:30.5`, `83.5`, `17|1`), and get every part's detune (with interval), key (Camelot, probability, runner-up), tempo and the differences between neighbours (significant or within the error, scale shift, tempo ratio). **Tempo** mode: varispeed or time-stretch verdict, the tempo where the track sits on A440, the detune / tempo curve plot with the suggested boundaries, the regions; typed tempo points or the host's tempo map ("use host tempo"). Progress bar and cancel (the analysis runs on its own thread); bad input says what is wrong; a recording that lost audio says so. Tested end to end (`centsible_beatswitch_plugin_test`: live recording through the real processor with a stand-in host, bar | beat, host tempo ramp, dropped file, cancel, bit-identical pass-through while recording).
- **Sections**: `centsible_cli --sections "0:45, 1:30.5"` (also `83.5`, `1:02:03.25`, bar|beat like `17|1` with `--bpm` or `--tempo-points`, or `--sections auto` to cut at the suggested boundaries). Every section is analysed on its own (0.75 s dropped around each boundary, sections under 8 s refused instead of guessed), and neighbours are compared: detune difference with its interval and whether it is significant, how far the scale moves, the total shift in cents, the tempo ratio.
- **Tempo automation**: `centsible_cli --automation`. Overlapping detune windows (3 s, widened where the evidence is thin) and a tempo track, then the verdict: **varispeed** (the pitch follows the tempo: detune = a + 1200 log2 (bpm / bpm_ref)), **tempo moves, pitch stays** (time-stretch), **pitch moves**, **constant**, or **mixed**. For a varispeed it names the tempo at which the track sits exactly on A440 (0 cents). Tempo points you type (`--tempo-points "0=120, 1:20=140"`, linear or steps) replace the measured tempo; the library also takes a host tempo map. Suggested boundaries (detune steps, tempo steps; short stretches that come back are marked as passages, not as beat switches), regions between them with detune, tempo, behaviour and key, and a curve CSV (`--curve-csv`).
- **Checked against exact truth** (REPORT.md section 7): 24 real songs played through a known speed ramp all read as varispeed with the reference tempo right (within 3 cents, median 1) and the detune change within 9.3 cents, 12 real-song splices all found (boundary within 1.2 s, each part within 0.6 cents of the song alone), 104 untouched songs: no suggested boundary in 68, mean 0.9 per song (the first version: 5.1); 104 synthetic checks.
- **Key readout**: the file readout of the plugin and the CLI show the key with its **Camelot code** (`A minor (8A)`), its probability, the **runner-up** and, when the relative major / minor pair cannot be told apart, the pair with its split (`A minor / C major, 60 / 40`). Per-section and per-region keys are measured against the tuning (a tuning curve inside a varispeed). Benchmarked: Krumhansl-Kessler, Temperley, Shaath / Albrecht-Shanahan / EDMA (the last three typed from memory): none beats the shipped two-stage detector (62 % exact, **85 % scale** now that a harmonic / percussive mask cleans the chroma, was 83 %; MIREX 0.734, was 0.728; tempo 98 % within 1 % as before); a second profile as extra evidence changed one song of 53, so it is not used.
- **Bigger catalog, honest numbers** (REPORT.md section 8): with 369 songs (was 106) the exact-shift consistency of the tuning is 88.4 % within 0.5 cents (the 13-track 98.5 % did not carry over to dense modern mixes); a few songs read a harmonic alias 13 - 15 cents away, some of them with a tight interval. The estimator is unchanged (the fits I tried on the larger catalog did not beat it). The key detector scores 45 % exact / 72 % scale on the songs it had never seen (62 % / 85 % on the ones it was designed on); its tonic weights are refitted on 140 labelled songs. Tables: `results/final_v12/`.
- Plugin: the key line of the dropped-file readout changed (above) and the panel was added; the audio path is untouched and still bit-identical (also while recording).
- Tools: `tools/beatswitch_real_eval.py` and `tests/beatswitch_eval.cpp` (untouched / `--varispeed` / `--splice` against exact truth), `tests/gen/warp_synth.h` (songs whose tempo and pitch move), `tests/gen/varispeed.h` (a real song through a speed ramp), `curve_probe`, `tools/key_profiles_bench.py`, `tools/key_stage2_as.py`.
- Installer: `dist\1.2.0\Centsible-Setup-1.2.0.exe` (per-version folder; v1.1.0's files stay where they were).
- Honest limits: a window of a real mix gives only a few cents of precision, so steps under 8 cents are not suggested; a step in tempo and pitch that happens to differ by whole semitones looks like a varispeed step (a ramp removes the doubt); without a steady pulse there is no tempo curve; "tempo moves, pitch stays" cannot tell a time-stretch from a tempo change in the music itself.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-06)

**Key and tempo detection rebuilt and measured against the QO & SPECTRE tracker** (62 of your songs matched with a tracker key, 83 with a tracker BPM):
- Key: **62 % exact (was 32 %)**, **83 % right scale / relative key (was 53 %)**, MIREX score 0.73 (was 0.53). New two-stage detector: scale from sustain-gated peak chroma and Temperley's profiles, then the tonic from the bass using iterative harmonic cancellation (so a bass note's overtone fifth is no longer mistaken for the key). Modal songs are named ("C minor (Phrygian)"), the relative key is shown ("A minor = C major"), and songs pitched about half a semitone off the grid are flagged as between two keys.
- Tempo: reported in 80-160 BPM like the tracker and most DJ tools, scored octave-agnostically: **98 % within 1 % of the tracker (was 80 %)**; the half / double reading is the alternative.
- Match mode aligns the notes' scale rather than the nominal key name.
- Research tooling: `key_frames`, `key_dump`, `tempo_dump`, `tools/key_*.py`, `tools/bpm_report.py` (see REPORT.md section 6).

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-03)

**Accuracy**
- Rebuilt as a single C++20 core (`core/`) shared by the plugin, the CLI and every test: a partial-aware maximum-likelihood fit on the 100-cent circle (fitted cluster width and roll-off, string stiffness, optional narrow + broad widths), bootstrap confidence interval, lock with hysteresis.
- Fixed: usually off by 1–2 cents, false "two tunings", +88 ¢ tracks reading "not pitched", answers that depended on how long the song had played.
- New: tuning drift is reported ("drifts +4.0 ¢ over the song"); slow drift is no longer mistaken for a new song.
- Honest limits: real audio 98.5 % within ±0.5 ¢ (first 3 minutes), two noisy tracks resolve only to about ±1 ¢ and say so; stretched-string bass below the tonal band is 0.12–0.17 ¢ off in synthetic tests. See `REPORT.md`.

**Plugin**
- Headline rounded to the nearest 5 ¢ (and labelled); exact value with its ± interval beside it; "shift … to fix" is exact and click-to-copy.
- Dual reading ("= +88.0 ¢ from one semitone down"), LOCKED state, history sparkline, hold / reset, music-reactive Chladni plate with toggle, resizable at a fixed aspect, drag and drop (one file: tuning, key, tempo; two files: match advice).
- Robustness: NaN / Inf input is replaced by silence instead of poisoning the analysis; metering and channel handling no longer trust the host; the analysis threads ask for a 2 MB stack and the core's working set is small (checked by running the tests on a 256 KB stack).
- New host-style stress test (22.05 – 192 kHz, block sizes 1 – 8192, mono / stereo, repeated prepare, reset mid-stream, NaN / Inf / clipping blocks).

**Tools**
- `centsible_cli`: batch folders, `--csv`, `--match`, key, BPM, drift.
- Windows installer (`dist\Centsible-Setup.exe`, Inno Setup).
